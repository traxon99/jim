Before building or changing any screen, overlay, popup, or input, read `docs/PWA.md` (from the
repo root). It collects the installed-iOS-PWA layout and scrolling rules this app learned from
shipped bugs.

Before shipping anything, read `docs/LESSONS.md` (from the repo root) and go through its pre-ship
checklist. When you fix a bug, add it to that file's ledger in the same PR.
